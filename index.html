About CareConnect

CareConnect is an academic web project developed for the CSC 3215 Web Technologies course at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). The platform aims to solve everyday difficulties faced by elderly individuals living alone by allowing them to post help requests for groceries, medicine pickup, and home repairs, while linked family guardians can monitor their safety remotely.

Our Core Services

Daily Errands & Groceries

Neighborhood volunteers assist with grocery shopping, prescription collection, and friendly visits.

Doctor Appointments

Schedule consultations with verified doctors, view digital prescriptions, and track medicine schedules.

Specialized Dietary Meals

Order fresh diabetic-friendly, low-salt, or soft meals from approved local food providers.

How It Works

1. Create Account

Register as a Care Seeker, Volunteer, Doctor, Guardian, or Provider.

2. Post or Accept Request

Care seekers post tasks; verified helpers in the neighborhood accept them.

3. Safe & Monitored

Tasks are completed with visit notes, and family guardians receive updates.
